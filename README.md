## System Overview

Terminal Lore centers on mysterious devices known as **Terminals**. These devices originate in the distant future and appear across earlier eras of Earth history.

Terminals are connected to **Chronolith**, an extraterrestrial mineral compound associated with spacetime instability, anomalous zones, temporal insertion, and warp-related technologies.

Across history, Terminals may be interpreted as sacred artifacts, mechanical instruments, computer terminals, control devices, or other era-appropriate objects through **Contextual Phase Adaptation**.

The universe explores a central question:

> What happens when humanity discovers technology it is not ready to understand?

## Archive Model

Terminal Lore uses two major archive layers.

### The Final Log

Machine-generated continuity index, technical wiki, and classification archive.

Use it for:

- explanations
- classification
- continuity
- concepts
- entities
- timelines
- unresolved questions

### The Book of Memories

Primary storytelling device made from recovered accounts, memories, logs, letters, recordings, songs, reports, and fragments.

Use it for:

- journals
- letters
- reports
- witness accounts
- field notes
- poems
- songs
- recovered media

**The Final Log** explains and classifies.

**The Book of Memories** tells through recovered memory.

This separation keeps the reference material useful without weakening the story format.

## The Final Log

[`final-log/`](final-log/) is the technical continuity archive of Terminal Lore.

In-universe, The Final Log was compiled at the terminal boundary of recorded time by an entity known as **The Null Archivist**.

It is not prophecy. It does not predict what will happen. It records what has already happened, what was recovered, what could be classified, and what could not be reconciled.

## The Book of Memories

[`book-of-memories/`](book-of-memories/) is reserved for Terminal Lore's primary storytelling format.

It should not read like a wiki.

It should contain the actual recovered artifacts of the universe:

- journals
- letters
- reports
- songs
- poems
- inscriptions
- radio transmissions
- field notes
- photographs
- machine logs
- witness testimony
- fragmented media

There are no prophecies in The Book of Memories. If a document appears predictive, it should be treated as a warning, theory, simulation, failed projection, or misunderstanding.

## Core Concepts

### Terminals

Time-displaced devices sent into earlier historical periods. They adapt to local expectations and are often misunderstood by the people who encounter them.

### Chronolith

A rare extraterrestrial mineral compound associated with spacetime instability, anomalous zones, temporal insertion, and warp-related systems.

### Contextual Phase Adaptation

The process that allows a Terminal to appear in a form appropriate to the technology and beliefs of the era where it is discovered.

### Horrox

A distant planetary system discovered by Horaccio Roxas using the Star Terminal. Later exploration and mining activity in the Horrox system becomes central to the catastrophe timeline.

### Specters

Mysterious cloaked or shrouded beings reported across history in accounts, photographs, and witness testimony. Their exact nature remains unresolved.

## Contributing

This is currently a personal creative project.

Future contributions should follow these principles:

- Keep the distinction between The Final Log and The Book of Memories clear.
- Use The Final Log for explanations, classification, and continuity.
- Use The Book of Memories for in-universe accounts and recovered artifacts.
- Preserve mystery where mystery improves the story.
- Document contradictions instead of hiding them.
- Keep changes focused and readable.
