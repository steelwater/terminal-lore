# Runic Labyrinth Simulator Record

**Recovered media type:** interactive simulation  
**Archive designation:** C.A.R.L. Simulator — Chronolith Adventure Runic Labyrinth  
**Historical scene:** St. Roch Cemetery, New Orleans, 1878  
**Later provenance:** New Orleans, 2101

---

## STORY FRAME

**St. Roch Cemetery, New Orleans, 1878.**

In the long age of ghouls, ghosts, fever, and mourning, a boy named Carl follows a luminous Orb into a sealed cemetery mausoleum.

The recovered simulator identifies **Dark4nal0g**, a programmer associated with the Chronolith Adventure Runic Labyrinth Simulator in New Orleans in **2101**.

---

## SIMULATION RECORD

**T00** — A strange Terminal awakens in Carl's hands. Its shell folds into a luminous Orb.

The Orb guides Carl toward a sealed mausoleum inside St. Roch Cemetery. Above the cemetery is a Full Moon. Below it are three recorded depths.

Carl enters.

Small Chronolith shards strengthen the Orb. Larger fragments restore its Light Aura. The Orb also maintains a Defensive Aura around Carl.

The labyrinth does not remain harmless.

Ghouls move through its passages. When the Orb's Light Aura is active, they flee from its light. Keys open the way into deeper chambers. Carl may retreat upward, and returning to the cemetery beneath the Full Moon restores the Orb's protective strength.

Deeper still, the simulator records a Demon guarding an unidentified relic.

The relic is represented as a glowing Rune.

Ordinary force cannot defeat the Demon. Carl must use the Orb's light.

When the Demon falls, the Rune begins to glow.

The Orb takes it.

The recovered system records the moment as the Rune being sealed inside the Orb's own light.

Carl climbs back through the three depths and returns to the cemetery.

The final surviving line states that after the Rune merged with the Orb, Carl's Runic Adventure ended and stories about the Orb seemed to be lost in time.

---

## ARCHIVE MARGIN

The simulator is not treated as a perfect recording.

Some details may be memory rendered as rules. Some may be reconstruction. Some may be literal. The maze regenerates; the names on tombstones vary; combat is quantified; the Orb gains levels. None of these properties alone proves that history behaved the same way.

But Carl, the cemetery, the Orb, the descent, the Chronolith shards, the Demon, the Rune, and the return survive every useful reading of the artifact.

And the simulator itself survives much later.

**New Orleans, 2101.**

A programmer calling himself **Dark4nal0g** tells Carl's story again during the rebuilding era after the technological collapse.

Whether he recovered it, inherited it, remembered it, or reconstructed it remains unrecorded.
