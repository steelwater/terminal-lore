# The Book of Memories

**The Book of Memories** is reserved as the primary storytelling archive for Terminal Lore.

It is not the wiki.

It is not the continuity index.

It is not a prophecy collection.

The Book of Memories should contain the actual recovered accounts: journals, letters, reports, songs, poems, inscriptions, radio transmissions, photographs, logs, field notes, witness testimony, and fragmented media.

## Relationship to The Final Log

- **The Final Log** explains and classifies.
- **The Book of Memories** tells through recovered memory.

The Final Log may point to the Book of Memories.

The Book of Memories should feel like the thing being recovered.
