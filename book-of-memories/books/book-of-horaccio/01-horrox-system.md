# Horrox System

Astronomical designation: **m.471**

The planet Horrox was discovered by astronomer Horaccio Roxas using the Star Terminal.

## Planet Horrox

Horrox is a rocky exoplanet several light-years from Earth.

Features:

- Mineral-rich crust
- Multiple moons
- No confirmed biological life

Horrox became the primary target for LightTech exploration missions.

## Moon: Minerva

Minerva was selected as the first mining site.

Characteristics:

- Rich in Hydronium Crystallite
- Heavy meteor impact history
- Extensive crater fields

Mining operations on Minerva were automated and unmanned. Ore shipments were transported to Mars for industrial refinement.

## Moon: M2

M2 is a second moon whose orbital velocity began accelerating unexpectedly.

Fragments from M2 were later deflected toward Earth, contributing to the global catastrophe.

## Horrox Orbital Station

A large orbital construction platform coordinated mining and probe missions, serving as a relay between Earth, Mars, and the Minerva mining complex.

## The Viajero Incident

The spacecraft Viajero attempted to reach Horrox orbital station after restoring Minerva operations. The vessel later malfunctioned and was lost near Horrox shortly before the M2 destabilization event.
