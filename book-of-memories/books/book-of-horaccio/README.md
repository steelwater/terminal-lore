# Book of Horaccio

The Book of Horaccio concerns the astronomer **Horaccio Roxas**, the discovery of Planet m.471, and the chain of events that led humanity toward Horrox.

Horrox was discovered using the **Star Terminal**. The planet later became the primary target for LightTech exploration and industrial expansion.

## Open Questions

- Was Horaccio the first true touched individual?
- Did the Star Terminal choose him, or was he merely the person who found it?
- Was the discovery of Horrox the cause of the catastrophe, or only one threshold in a larger system?
