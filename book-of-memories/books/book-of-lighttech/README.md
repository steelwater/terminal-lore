# Book of LightTech

The Book of LightTech collects corporate reports, technical summaries, and failure analyses from LightTech Corporation's Temporal Systems Division.

LightTech attempted to alter the projected future through temporal intervention. Its strategy depended on suppressing or changing the discovery chain leading to Horrox.

The surviving reports suggest that LightTech succeeded in deploying Terminals into earlier eras, but failed to meaningfully alter the catastrophe timeline.
