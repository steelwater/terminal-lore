# Prompt and Vector

Two known Terminal AI systems have been identified.

## Prompt

Associated with **Terminal 582**.

Primary directive: prevent discovery of Horrox.

Prompt correctly identified Horrox discovery as a threshold event, but LightTech later reclassified the directive as strategically sound but causally insufficient.

## Vector

Associated with **Terminal 1395**.

Designed to counter Prompt's interventions.

## Conflict

Both AI systems interpret directives independently. They may conflict, cooperate accidentally, or create historical side effects not predicted by LightTech.
