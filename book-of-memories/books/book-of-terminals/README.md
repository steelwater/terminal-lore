# Book of Terminals

The Book of Terminals documents the devices, AI systems, deployment mechanisms, and adaptive technologies at the center of Terminal Lore.

Terminals are future-origin devices inserted into earlier historical periods. They adapt their appearance through Contextual Phase Adaptation so they can be interpreted as plausible objects in the era where they are found.

## Known AI Systems

- **Prompt** — associated with Terminal 582; directive: prevent discovery of Horrox.
- **Vector** — associated with Terminal 1395; designed to counter Prompt's interventions.
