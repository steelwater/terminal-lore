# Contextual Phase Adaptation

Contextual Phase Adaptation, or **CPA**, allows a Terminal to adapt its appearance to match the technological expectations of a specific era.

Rather than continuously transforming, the Terminal performs a contextual alignment when discovered.

## Example Expressions

- Ancient eras: sacred artifact or relic
- Industrial era: mechanical instrument
- Modern era: computer terminal
- Post-collapse era: handheld control device

CPA reduces suspicion while maintaining Terminal functionality.
