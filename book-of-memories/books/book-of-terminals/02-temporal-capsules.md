# Temporal Capsules

Temporal capsules are containment systems used to send Terminals into earlier historical periods.

## Functions

- Chronolith Resonant reactor
- Temporal insertion stabilization
- Environmental protection over centuries

Capsules are typically hidden inside anomalous zones where spacetime instability allows temporal displacement.
