# Book of Origins

The Book of Origins explains the basic premise of Terminal Lore.

Terminal Lore is a science-fiction universe centered around mysterious computer devices known as **Terminals**. These devices originate in the distant future and were sent backward through time using anomalous zones created by an extraterrestrial mineral called **Chronolith**.

Throughout history, Terminals appear in forms appropriate to their era through a system called **Contextual Phase Adaptation**. This allows them to blend into ancient, industrial, modern, or post-collapse environments while retaining their deeper function.

The story spans ancient civilizations, pirate legends, World War discoveries, and a distant future where humanity discovers the planet **Horrox**. Mining operations on Horrox's moon **Minerva** introduce new Chronolith materials that enable warp travel, temporal technologies, and industrial expansion beyond Earth.

However, the spread of Chronolith through space may have awakened or alerted an unknown extraterrestrial intelligence.

Terminal Lore explores the consequences of humanity discovering technologies it does not fully understand while trying to change its own future.
