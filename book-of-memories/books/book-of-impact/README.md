# Book of Impact

The Book of Impact preserves ideological documents and warnings attributed to the **Sons of Impact**.

The Sons of Impact believe the meteor was not an accident. They interpret Chronolith disturbance as a signal or activation event, and they accuse LightTech of choosing profit over preparation.

Their writings frame humanity as having entered a cosmic system older than itself.
