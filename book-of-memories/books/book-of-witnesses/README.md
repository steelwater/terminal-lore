# Book of Witnesses

The Book of Witnesses is for people, factions, or communities touched by Terminals across history.

This is where the new book-based structure becomes strongest: each Terminal encounter can produce a witness account. The witness may be the discoverer, the keeper, the destroyer, the interpreter, or the victim of the Terminal's influence.

## Known / Planned Witness Threads

- Ancient temple inscriptions describing anomalous devices
- Pirate journals describing a metallic capsule containing a thinking machine
- Mid-20th century military documents referencing unidentified temporal devices
- Horaccio Roxas and the Star Terminal
- Echo Squad / The Lost Legion

## Design Rule

A witness book should not only explain what happened. It should show how the person misunderstood the Terminal according to their era.
