# Book of Continuity

The Book of Continuity tracks canon changes, open questions, contradictions, and future lore work.

## Current Major Open Questions

- Name of the meteor mineral that activates anomalous zones, if different from Chronolith Resonant
- Chronolith degradation stages
- Alien activation mechanism
- Exact origin of anomalous zones
- Fate of Echo Squad / The Lost Legion
- Timeline of Sons of Impact formation
- Terminal discovery events across ancient civilizations
- Whether each Book is named after a person, faction, Terminal, or recovered archive source

## Continuity Rule

When in doubt, preserve mystery but document the question.
