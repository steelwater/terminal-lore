# Chronolith Prime

Chronolith Prime is the earliest known Chronolith form in Terminal Lore.

It is described as a stable crystalline lattice that is inert to ordinary chemical reactions, yet capable of creating long-term spacetime stress fields.

## Effects

- Formation of anomalous zones on Earth
- Geological instability
- Planetary-scale spacetime distortion
- Long-term fractures in local spacetime

Chronolith Prime fractures spacetime but does not actively manipulate it.
