# Book of Chronolith

The Book of Chronolith documents the extraterrestrial mineral compound responsible for spacetime instability, anomalous zones on Earth, temporal insertion, and warp systems.

Chronolith behaves like a spacetime catalyst. It alters the topology of space rather than serving as a traditional fuel or energy source.

## Known Forms

- **Chronolith Prime** — ancient, stable, inert, responsible for long-term spacetime fractures on Earth.
- **Chronolith Residue** — microscopic degraded fragments that stabilize warp systems and spread through probes, ships, and equipment.
- **Chronolith Resonant** — evolved/reactive compound from a later meteor, used by LightTech temporal systems and Terminal deployment capsules.

## Core Mystery

Chronolith may not merely be material. It may be part of a detection, activation, or response system beyond human understanding.
