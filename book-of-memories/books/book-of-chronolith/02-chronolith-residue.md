# Chronolith Residue

Chronolith Residue consists of microscopic fragments degraded from Chronolith Prime.

It is chemically inert and extremely low in mass, but it reacts with high-energy spacetime fields. Humanity later used it, knowingly and unknowingly, in warp stabilization systems and long-range exploratory technologies.

## Effects

- Stabilizes warp systems
- Enables interstellar transit through spacetime fractures
- Leaves temporal signatures detectable by advanced sensors
- Propagates beyond the Sol system through spacecraft and mining equipment

LightTech originally classified residue contamination as harmless. That classification was later revoked.
