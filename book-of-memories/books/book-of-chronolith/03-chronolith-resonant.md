# Chronolith Resonant

Chronolith Resonant originated from a modern meteor containing an evolved Chronolith compound.

It is highly reactive to energy and resonates with existing spacetime fractures. It amplified dormant anomalous zones and became the primary material used in LightTech temporal systems.

## Effects

- Enables temporal capsule insertion
- Activates dormant anomalous zones
- Allows Contextual Phase Adaptation systems to function
- Amplifies anomalous zone activity

Chronolith Resonant is the material that made Terminal deployment into earlier eras possible.
