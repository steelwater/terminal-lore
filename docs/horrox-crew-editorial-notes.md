# Horrox founding crew — editorial notes

**Maintainer-only reference, outside the in-universe personnel dossiers.** The material below preserves original author qualifications, unconfirmed relationships, character development directions and story possibilities. The owner reviewed all nine individual biographies without requesting content changes.

## Continuity and access constraints

- The dossiers retain `status: draft` frontmatter until an explicit canon decision.
- 1st Horrox Mining Ltd. begins staffed operations on **2075-09-17**. Prior career appointment dates, exact birth dates, callsign origin dates and later advancement are not fixed.
- The names **the Kettle** and **Fran** are earned later; at opening, it is the Minerva Mining Facility and its **Facility AI / Control**. Neither is a human crew member.
- Crew in 2075 do not know the mineral's later identification as Chronolith, M2's fate, or future terminal research. The first person to identify a recurring anomalous mineral pattern is still unresolved.
- No story hook here creates a recorded injury, commendation, trust change, ship transfer, discovery, or accident. Player-specific histories belong to individual saves.
- `steady`, `bright` and `measured` are current **software TTS voice profile IDs**, not ethnicity, accent or final casting. The existing assignments remain in [roster.json](../final-log/personnel/horrox/roster.json).
- Dossier biography is not the same thing as captain-facing permission; opening-day data remains restricted by the allowlist in [SCHEMA.md](../final-log/personnel/SCHEMA.md).
- The original homeworld, age, education, language, family details, relationships beyond implemented pairs, and story hooks were authored as proposed material. No metadata in the in-universe dossier body should discuss implementation or approval.

## Original notes by crew member

### [Mara Vale — Latch](../final-log/personnel/horrox/mara-vale.md)

**Relationship interpretation, including unconfirmed details:** Latch and Patch trust each other's judgments because neither hides an error behind a cheerful report. That trust is established; the proposed history of repairing a badly labeled approach beacon together is NOT a confirmed event. Mara initially finds Patch's spoken thought process distracting, but appreciates knowing where the engineer's mind has gone.

**Confidential author/development note:** Protectiveness can become micromanagement. Proposed future arc: she learns to let Patch or a junior controller own a difficult but safe decision while she supports instead of intercepting.

**Future hook — not established history:** FUTURE / UNCONFIRMED: a harmless-looking mismatch between a berth schedule and cargo log forces Mara to choose between delaying a hauler and explaining the discrepancy to Control; no incident or outcome is pre-written.

**Original qualification labels:** docking, traffic control, external repairs; languages: **Cebuano, English; working Tagalog** (proposed proficiencies; actual native/casting accents UNKNOWN)..
