## Character and personal history — PROPOSED / OWNER REVIEW

Bea has handled enough heavy loads to distrust any claim that a cargo move is 'just one quick lift.' She likes workers who say exactly what they do not know, and machines that behave in ways a tired person can predict. Cargo Mechs are equipment under supervision—not pals, minds, or substitutes for responsibility.

Her joke arrives while the machinery is safely stationary. During a lift, her speech becomes plain and serious. The contradiction is useful: she can sound dismissive of a fancy new tool but spend an entire rest interval studying how to operate it correctly. Her blind spot is hiding anxiety behind a quip when the crew might benefit from knowing the concern directly.

### Private life, habits and ambitions — PROPOSED

Builds tiny wooden and metal lifting rigs as puzzles and sends pictures to a niece learning physics on Earth. She is proud of a past reputation for stopping a job instead of improvising unsafe rigging, but the exact incident and date remain deliberately undefined.
