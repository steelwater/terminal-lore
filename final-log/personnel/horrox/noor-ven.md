## Character and personal history — PROPOSED / OWNER REVIEW

Noor is not quiet because there is nothing to say. Facet waits until a judgment can survive the questions it will provoke. During earlier terrestrial work, a disputed classification was eventually corrected by retrieving the original sample; the lesson was not that Noor had been right, but that the evidence had to remain retrievable.

Noor's reserve is an asset when money or reputation depends on a grade. It can also become a weakness: an early warning delivered late is still late. Beneath the measured distance is an appetite for puzzles, provided the rules are visible and the pieces honest.

### Private life, habits and ambitions — PROPOSED

Enjoys old rule-bound games, including text-only ones, and writes carefully indexed notes on them. Keeps small recordings of family gatherings but does not volunteer them to coworkers. Aspires to teach grading apprentices how to dissent without embarrassing anyone.

### Relationships and tension

PROPOSED: Facet respects Sieve's careful process but is not automatically persuaded by it. The existing TS-06 exchange with Crate establishes a dry response to old games, not a broader friendship history.
