# LightTech Corporation

LightTech Corporation is the organization responsible for major Chronolith research, Horrox exploration, and temporal intervention programs.

Its Temporal Systems Division attempted to alter the projected future by deploying CPA Terminals into earlier historical periods.

## Known Programs

- Chronolith classification
- Temporal capsule systems
- Terminal deployment
- Horrox probe missions
- Minerva mining coordination
- Post-Horrox failure analysis

## Final Log Assessment

LightTech successfully deployed Terminals into earlier eras but failed to meaningfully alter the catastrophe timeline.

The failure was not simply technological.

It was causal, historical, and systemic.
