# Sons of Impact

The Sons of Impact are an ideological faction formed around the belief that the meteor was not an accident.

They believe humanity exists inside a cosmic system older than the species itself, and that Chronolith disturbance may have drawn attention from something beyond ordinary detection.

## Core Claims

- The meteor was a signal.
- LightTech chose profit over preparation.
- Mining Minerva awakened or activated something.
- Warp travel spreads Chronolith contamination across space.
- Humanity may already have been noticed.

## Archive Note

Their manifesto belongs to the Book of Memories as an artifact.

The Final Log classifies the faction and its claims.
