# Horrox System

Astronomical designation: **m.471**

The planet Horrox was discovered by astronomer Horaccio Roxas using the Star Terminal.

## Planet Horrox

Rocky exoplanet several light-years from Earth.

Known features:

- mineral-rich crust
- multiple moons
- no confirmed biological life

## Minerva

Minerva was selected as the first mining site.

Known features:

- Hydronium Crystallite deposits
- heavy meteor impact history
- extensive crater fields
- automated mining operations

## M2

M2 is a second moon whose orbital velocity began accelerating unexpectedly.

Fragments from M2 later contributed to the catastrophe timeline.

## Viajero Incident

The spacecraft Viajero attempted to reach Horrox orbital station after restoring Minerva operations. The vessel malfunctioned and was lost near Horrox shortly before the M2 destabilization event.
