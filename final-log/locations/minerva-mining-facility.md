# Minerva Mining Facility

The Minerva mining facility is the principal industrial base associated with mining operations around Horrox's first moon.

## Operational Eras

- **2054-11-04** — LightTech establishes the first unmanned mining facility on Minerva.
- **2065-05-14** — communications and ore shipments cease.
- **2073-07-06** — Viajero reaches Minerva and restarts mining operations.
- **2075-09-17** — 1st Horrox Mining Ltd. begins staffed operations.

The staffed facility supports mining vessels working the Minerva Cluster and handles refining, grading, storage, maintenance, docking, Cargo Mech operations, and Hydronium export to Mars.

## Identity

At Launch Day the facility has not yet acquired its later informal identity.

The facility AI is initially known as **Facility AI / Control** and only later becomes known to the crew as **Fran**.

The facility itself only later acquires the nickname **the Kettle**, following the Kettle Incident and repeated crew retellings.

## Life at the Facility

The facility is a workplace and community. Its personnel include miners and crews associated with Refinery, Grading, Storage, Cargo, Docking, Maintenance, visiting transports, and other support operations.

Its shared off-shift recreation space is known as **the Cantina**.

The operation maintains a formal Operations Handbook and an old-industry-style safety record tracking consecutive station-days without a recordable safety accident.

## Chronolith

Hydronium is the facility's established commercial resource. Tiny fragments of an unknown material occur within some Hydronium-bearing asteroids but are invisible to standard mineral scanners.

Recurring excavator breakdowns and interference provide the first practical clue. A crew member eventually notices that some otherwise unexplained faults correlate with particular MNV targets or ore batches. Investigation isolates small samples, which receive an ordinary mineral/sample designation rather than the name Chronolith.

A major concentration of the same numbered material is discovered later on M2. Its important properties remain unknown at first. Only later history identifies it as Chronolith and connects deliberate extraction to the catastrophe.

The mineral number, discovering crew member, exact M2 deposit, dates, and precise causal mechanism remain unresolved.
