# Terminals

Terminals are future-origin devices inserted into earlier historical periods.

They adapt to local expectations through **Contextual Phase Adaptation** and may be interpreted as sacred artifacts, mechanical instruments, computer terminals, control devices, or other era-appropriate objects.

## Known Properties

- Sent backward through time by temporal capsule systems
- Associated with Chronolith Resonant technology
- Difficult or impossible to recover after deployment
- Often preserved in historical records as misunderstood objects
- May contain or connect to independent AI systems

## Known AI Systems

- **Prompt** — associated with Terminal 582
- **Vector** — associated with Terminal 1395

## Recovered Terminal Designations

Older source layers preserve additional Terminal names and models. Their dates and relationships are not yet fully reconciled with the future-origin Terminal system described elsewhere in the Final Log.

### Alpha Terminal

A 1940 Earth record describes Alpha Terminal as being written in Assembly Language.

Status: `draft`

### Terminal 0

A 1954 Earth record describes Terminal 0 as being written in BASIC.

Status: `draft`

### Terminal 1

A 1980 Earth record describes Terminal 1 as a minimalist maintenance terminal using a text menu interface to connect to networks and computers.

Status: `draft`

### Star Terminal

Associated with Horaccio Roxas and the discovery of **Planet m.471**, known as Horrox.

Recovered sources disagree on whether the key discovery belongs to 1992, 2016, or the 2020 public announcement.

Status: `contradiction` chronology

### Terminal 582

Associated with Prompt and LightTech's attempt to interfere with the Star Terminal discovery of Planet m.471 through temporal deployment.

A recovered source places its deployment in 3201 / the 33rd century.

Status: `canon` identity, `contradiction` chronology details

### Terminal 684

Listed in a 1996 record as the **Mediculo Terminal**. No further technical description survives in the recovered timeline.

Status: `draft`

### Terminal 934 Model

A handheld computer associated with Gatherer drones in South Africa in **2138**.

Recovered specifications:

- monochrome text-based display
- 32-bit CPU
- 100 MHz clock speed
- 4 GB memory
- portable drone-control role

Status: `draft`

### Valhalla Terminal

Listed in an unknown future period. The recovered Terminal Timeline provides no additional description.

Other Terminal Lore material associates the Valhalla Terminal with the **Valhalla Rain Simulation Program**, though whether that program was only a simulation remains unresolved.

Status: `draft`

### pHT — Personnel HyperSpace Terminal

A space-service terminal used to monitor personnel vital statistics aboard spacecraft.

The Viajero crew modified a pHT to interface with ship computers, retrieve information, control ship functions, and connect to the Horrox orbital station.

Status: `draft`
