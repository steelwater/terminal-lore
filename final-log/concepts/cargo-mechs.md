# Cargo Mechs

**Cargo Mechs** are heavy industrial machines used at the staffed Minerva mining facility.

Under Cargo crew supervision they:

- retrieve containers from storage
- move Hydronium through cargo decks
- load and unload Mars transports
- reposition heavy equipment
- assist Maintenance with large components

Cargo Mechs are **not sentient characters**.

Individual machines may nevertheless acquire reputations through repeated maintenance quirks and crew chatter. A troublesome actuator, noisy hydraulic system, awkward handling characteristic, or recurring fault can become part of facility folklore without implying machine consciousness.
