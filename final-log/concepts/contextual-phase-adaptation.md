# Contextual Phase Adaptation

Contextual Phase Adaptation, or **CPA**, allows a Terminal to adapt its appearance to the technological expectations of a specific era.

The process is not continuous shapeshifting. It is a contextual alignment that occurs when the Terminal is discovered or activated in a new historical setting.

## Example Classifications

- Ancient eras: sacred artifact or relic
- Industrial era: mechanical instrument
- Modern era: computer terminal
- Post-collapse era: handheld control device

CPA reduces suspicion while maintaining Terminal functionality.
