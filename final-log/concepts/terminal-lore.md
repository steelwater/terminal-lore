# Terminal Lore

Terminal Lore is a science fiction universe centered around mysterious devices known as **Terminals**.

The Terminals originate in the distant future and were sent backward through time using anomalous zones connected to **Chronolith**, an extraterrestrial mineral compound associated with spacetime instability.

Throughout history, Terminals appear in forms appropriate to their era through **Contextual Phase Adaptation**.

Documented or suspected Terminal-related records include ancient inscriptions, pirate journals, military documents, corporate reports, machine logs, and personal accounts.

The Final Log classifies these materials.

The Book of Memories preserves them as story artifacts.
