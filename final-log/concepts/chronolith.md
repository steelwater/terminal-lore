# Chronolith

Chronolith is a rare extraterrestrial mineral compound responsible for spacetime instability, anomalous zones on Earth, temporal insertion, and warp-related technologies.

Chronolith behaves like a spacetime catalyst. It alters the topology of space rather than serving as a conventional fuel source.

## Known Forms

### Chronolith Prime

Ancient, stable, inert. Associated with long-term spacetime stress fields and the formation of anomalous zones.

### Chronolith Residue

Microscopic degraded fragments. Associated with warp stabilization and unintended propagation through spacecraft, probes, and equipment.

### Chronolith Resonant

Reactive compound associated with temporal capsule insertion, dormant zone activation, and Contextual Phase Adaptation systems.

## Classification Note

The origin of Chronolith remains unknown.
