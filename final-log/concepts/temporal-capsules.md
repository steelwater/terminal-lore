# Temporal Capsules

Temporal capsules are containment systems used to send Terminals into earlier historical periods.

## Known Functions

- Chronolith Resonant reactor system
- Temporal insertion stabilization
- Long-duration environmental protection
- Concealment inside anomalous zones

Capsules are typically hidden in areas where spacetime instability allows displacement across historical periods.
