# Specters

Specters are mysterious beings reported across history in written accounts, photographs, and witness testimony.

They are commonly described as cloaked, shrouded, incomplete, or ethereal. Their forms vary in size and number. They may appear alone or in groups.

## Known Interpretations

- ghosts
- wraiths
- angels
- extraterrestrial visitors
- one entity appearing as many

## Recorded Accounts

A captain logged a Specter sighting aboard the ship **The Behemoth**, bound for Horrox.

The Touched have written more than one account of Specter encounters. One Touched account suggests that all Specters may be manifestations of a single entity.

## Classification Note

Specters belong in the Final Log as a concept, but their strongest story treatment belongs in the Book of Memories through witness accounts, logs, and recovered records.
