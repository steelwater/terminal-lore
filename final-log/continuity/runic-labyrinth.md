# Runic Labyrinth

Runic Labyrinth is a recovered historical reconstruction presented through the **C.A.R.L. Simulator — Chronolith Adventure Runic Labyrinth**.

The simulator preserves two chronological layers:

1. **Carl's encounter in New Orleans in 1878**.
2. **The later simulator record associated with Dark4nal0g in New Orleans in 2062**.

> **NULL ARCHIVIST // SOURCE NOTICE**  
> The archive does not assume that every rule inside a simulation is a literal historical event. The C.A.R.L. Simulator may contain memory, reconstruction, interpretation, compression, or mechanics created to make an incomplete event navigable. What the simulator shows is preserved. What actually happened remains a separate question.

## Historical Layer — 1878

**Location:** St. Roch Cemetery, New Orleans  
**Date:** 1878

The published simulator introduces Carl as a boy living during an age marked by ghouls, ghosts, fever, and mourning.

Carl follows a luminous Orb into a sealed cemetery mausoleum.

Inside the simulator, the encounter unfolds as follows:

- A strange Terminal awakens in Carl's hands and its shell folds into a luminous Orb.
- The Orb guides Carl toward a sealed mausoleum in St. Roch Cemetery.
- The mausoleum descends through **three recorded depths**.
- Small Chronolith shards are consumed by the Orb and increase its strength.
- Large Chronolith shards restore charges for the Orb's **Light Aura**.
- The Full Moon above the cemetery restores the Orb's Light Aura charges and Defensive Aura when Carl retreats to the surface.
- Ghouls inhabit the lower passages and flee from the activated Light Aura.
- Carl must recover keys to continue through deeper passages.
- At the lowest recorded depth, a Demon guards an unidentified glowing Rune or relic sought by the Orb.
- Ordinary attacks cannot harm the Demon; the simulator requires the Orb's Light Aura.
- After the Demon falls, the Rune becomes accessible.
- The Orb seals the Rune inside its own light.
- Carl returns through the labyrinth to the cemetery.
- The simulator closes the event by stating that Carl's Runic Adventure ended and stories of the Orb seemed to be lost in time.

## Future Layer — 2062

**Location:** New Orleans  
**Date:** 2062  
**Associated figure:** Dark4nal0g

The current published simulator source identifies **Dark4nal0g** as a programmer in New Orleans in 2062 who tells Carl's story through the Chronolith Adventure Runic Labyrinth Simulator.

Earlier preserved builds state that **DarkAnal0g created the simulator**. Those earlier builds also pass through an attribution to **One Touched** before the later DarkAnal0g / Dark4nal0g wording appears.

The Final Log therefore uses **2062, New Orleans** as the current provenance anchor for the C.A.R.L. Simulator while leaving the exact construction date and the relationship between Dark4nal0g and the Touched unresolved.

> **NULL ARCHIVIST // ATTRIBUTION NOTICE**  
> The surviving builds do not agree on the name attached to the reconstruction. Some remember One Touched. Others remember DarkAnal0g. The latest recovered form names Dark4nal0g and places him in New Orleans in 2062. The archive records the disagreement because authorship may itself be part of what the simulator changed, inherited, or remembered.

## Relationship to the Technological Collapse

The existing Terminal Lore chronology currently places the M2 impact and global technological collapse in **2083**, followed by a rebuilding period.

The published C.A.R.L. source places Dark4nal0g and the simulator in **2062**, twenty-one years before that event.

This does not make the records impossible, but it creates an important continuity question because the technological setback is a core historical anchor for Runic Labyrinth.

Until the chronology is refined, the archive preserves both statements:

- **2062 — New Orleans:** the current simulator provenance record.
- **2083 — Earth:** the M2-related technological collapse.

If the C.A.R.L. Simulator is ultimately intended to belong to the post-collapse rebuilding culture, its exact year will require reconciliation rather than silent correction.
