# Timeline

This timeline is a technical continuity index, not a narrative sequence.

> **NULL ARCHIVIST // CHRONOLOGY NOTICE**  
> Multiple surviving sources record different dates for some of the same events. The archive preserves those differences rather than silently selecting a preferred version. Entries marked `contradiction` require later reconciliation; entries marked `draft` are usable but may move as the chronology develops.

For the fuller recovered chronology and its source conflicts, see [Recovered Terminal Timeline](recovered-terminal-timeline.md).

## Ancient Chronolith Event

Chronolith Prime arrives on Earth and creates long-term spacetime stress fields and anomalous zones.

Status: `canon`

## Modern Chronolith Resonant Event

Chronolith Resonant reactivates dormant anomalous zones and makes later temporal systems possible.

Status: `canon`

## 1878 — Carl's Runic Labyrinth Encounter

At St. Roch Cemetery in New Orleans, a boy named Carl follows a luminous Orb into a sealed cemetery mausoleum.

The surviving C.A.R.L. Simulator reconstructs three lower depths containing Chronolith shards, ghouls, keys and passages, a Demon, and a glowing Rune or relic. The Orb ultimately takes the Rune and Carl returns to the cemetery.

Because the event is known through a later simulator, the archive distinguishes the stable story anchors from mechanics that may be reconstruction rather than literal history.

See [Runic Labyrinth](runic-labyrinth.md).

Status: `draft`

Terminal 0 is described as an Earth-based system written in BASIC.

## 1980 — Terminal 1 Record

## 1992 / 2016 / 2020 — Discovery of Horrox

Horaccio Roxas discovers Planet m.471, later known as Horrox, using the Star Terminal. One record places a Star Terminal discovery in 1992; another describes discovery in 2016; the fuller chronology places the public announcement on 2020-02-16.

Status: `contradiction`

## 2062 — C.A.R.L. Simulator Provenance

The current Runic Labyrinth source identifies **Dark4nal0g**, a programmer in New Orleans in 2062, as the person telling Carl's 1878 story through the **Chronolith Adventure Runic Labyrinth Simulator**.

Earlier preserved builds say DarkAnal0g created the simulator, while still earlier versions attribute the reconstruction to One Touched. The exact construction date therefore remains provisional, but **New Orleans / 2062** is the current provenance anchor.

See [Runic Labyrinth](runic-labyrinth.md) and [Dark4nal0g](../entities/dark4nal0g.md).

Status: `draft`

## 2065-05-14 — Minerva Falls Silent

Communications and ore shipments from Minerva stop. Later probes fail to establish a confirmed cause.

## 2067–2068 — Viajero Mission Prepared and Launched

A first long-distance human crew trains for Horrox. Viajero launches in 2068 with a crew described as convicted felons offered freedom and a life on Minerva.

## 2073-07-06 — Viajero Reaches Minerva

## 2082-03-09 — Nuclear Deflection Attempt

Status: `draft`

## 2083 — M2 Impact and Technological Collapse

Remaining fragments strike Earth over an extended period. Recovered records describe worldwide geological disturbance, volcanic eruptions, a catastrophic electromagnetic failure, and the collapse of electronics and machine-dependent infrastructure.

The world enters a technological dark age. Surviving books and accumulated knowledge later allow humanity to rebuild.

This is a core historical anchor for **Runic Labyrinth**.

The current Runic Labyrinth simulator provenance record is dated 2062, before this collapse. If the simulator is later established as a post-collapse reconstruction, its date will require reconciliation.

See [M2 Impact and Technological Collapse](m2-impact-and-technological-collapse.md) and [Runic Labyrinth](runic-labyrinth.md).

Status: `draft`

## 2113 — 8086B Rebuilding-Era Chip

The recovered chronology records the creation and widespread use of an 8086B chip during the technological rebuilding period.

Status: `draft`

## 2038 / 2138 — Terminal 934 and Gatherers

Records place Terminal 934 and Gatherer deployment in South Africa in two different centuries. Gatherers are described as small mining drones used to collect meteor fragments for sale to refineries.

Status: `contradiction`

## 3201 / 33rd Century — Terminal 582 Deployment

LightTech places Terminal 582 inside a temporal capsule in a classified anomalous zone. Prompt is associated with the mission to prevent the Star Terminal discovery.

Recovered sources disagree over whether the target designation was M.456 or m.471 / Horrox.

Status: `contradiction`

## Later Terminal Deployments

LightTech deploys CPA Terminals into earlier historical eras.
