# Recovered Terminal Timeline

This entry preserves lore from the older **Terminal Timeline** working document.

> **NULL ARCHIVIST // DISCREPANCY NOTICE**  
> The surviving records do not agree on every year, designation, or sequence. Some were written close to the events; others were copied, reconstructed, or remembered much later. Multiple sources therefore describe the same history from different positions of knowledge. Dates below are preserved rather than forced into a single chronology. Where records disagree, the disagreement is part of the archive until better evidence is recovered.

This source contains material that predates the current Final Log structure. It is retained because a disputed record is still a record.

## Loose Fragments Preserved From the Source

The original document begins with undeveloped lore fragments. Their relationships to the main chronology remain unresolved:

- Patagonia rock art
- no water on Mars
- end of dinosaurs
- cockroaches
- a beholder-like alien full of tongues
- anomalous human DNA / gene material found underground on Mars
- non-coding DNA found in introns in a DNA sequence

These fragments are not reconciled canon. They are preserved for later development.

## Early Terminal Records

### 1940 — Alpha Terminal

**Where:** Earth

Alpha Terminal was described as being written in Assembly Language.

### 1954 — Terminal 0

**Where:** Earth

Terminal 0 was described as being written in BASIC.

### 1980 — Terminal 1

**Where:** Earth

Terminal 1 was a minimalist computer-maintenance terminal. It used a text menu interface to connect to different networks and computers.

### 1992 — Conflicting Terminal Record

One navigation fragment lists **Terminal 582** in 1992. Elsewhere in the same source, **Star Terminal** is the device associated with the Philippine astronomical discovery record.

> **Archive discrepancy:** The surviving source does not establish whether the 1992 Terminal 582 reference is a mistaken label, a separate encounter, or evidence that later records merged two devices.

### 1996 — Terminal 684

Terminal 684 is listed as the **Mediculo Terminal**. No further description survives in this source.

### 2038 — Terminal 934

A summary fragment places Terminal 934 in South Africa in 2038 and says merchants used it to deploy Gatherers.

A later, more detailed entry places this activity in **2138**.

> **Archive discrepancy:** Both dates are retained. The records may refer to different models, different deployments, or an incorrect copied century.

### Unknown Future — Valhalla Terminal

The Valhalla Terminal is listed without further description in this source.

### Unknown Future — pHT

The **Personnel HyperSpace Terminal (pHT)** was used by space-service personnel to monitor vital statistics while working aboard spacecraft. The Viajero crew later modified a pHT to interface with ship computers, retrieve information, control ship functions, and connect to the Horrox orbital station.

# Horrox Expansion Chronology

## 2016 — Discovery Record

Astronomer **Horaccio Roxas**, working with the **Star Terminal**, detected a repeating signal or pattern involving meteors passing through Earth's orbit. The Star Terminal helped identify coordinates encoded in the repeating pattern. Those coordinates pointed to a planet designated **m.471**.

> **Archive discrepancy:** Other surviving records place the Star Terminal discovery in 1992, while this source separately records a public announcement in 2020. The Final Log therefore treats the exact discovery year as unresolved.

## 2020-02-16 — Public Announcement

Horaccio Roxas, described in this source as a backyard Filipino astronomer, officially announced the discovery of m.471 through his YouTube channel.

## 2020-07-01 — Early Prompt Prototype

A very early prototype of the program that would become associated with **Prompt** was tested and compiled in the laboratory of the Calinan Technology Institute in the Philippines.

The source itself leaves the program's early name unwritten.

## 2021-02-16 — Horrox Accepted

On the first anniversary of the public announcement, astronomers were said to have accepted the existence of m.471 and named the planet **Horrox**.

## 2021-03-01 — LightTech Probe Funding

Private mining corporation **LightTech Corp.** funded a probe project to explore Horrox.

## 2022–2029 — Mars Expansion

A Mars space program was created and successfully launched. A research facility, small mining operation, and Mars colony were established.

By the middle of the century, industrial activity was increasingly moved to Mars. The source states that this contributed to environmental recovery on Earth.

## 2029-08-14 — Horrox Probe Launch

An unnamed probe was launched toward Horrox on a planned seven-year journey.

The probe deployed beacons along its path to serve as relay stations for faster communication.

## 2036-08-14 — Probe Reaches Horrox

The probe reached Horrox and reported:

- a mineral-rich planet suitable for mining
- no detected planetary life
- two moons

## 2039-09-06 — Horrox Satellite Platform

A satellite platform was created to orbit Horrox. The source says it was created and launched from an orbital platform associated with Earth.

## 2041 — Extinct Animal Zoo

A zoo was created for extinct animals restored through cloning. Dinosaurs and animals considered significant threats to human life were excluded.

## 2054-11-04 — Minerva Mining Station

The first mining station was established on Horrox's first moon, **Minerva**.

It was described as the first unmanned mining facility in the system and shipped **Hydronium ore** to Mars. The facility was built, owned, and operated for profit by LightTech Corp.

The source describes Mars as increasingly industrial by this period, with Earth becoming greener and more livable as heavy industry moved away from it.

## 2056 — Horrox Orbital Station

An orbital space station was built to support the creation and coordination of mining operations around Horrox and its smaller moons.

## 2065-05-14 — Minerva Falls Silent

The Minerva mine stopped transmitting communications and ore shipments ceased.

Subsequent probes could not determine the cause. Speculation and anomalous probe footage led some observers to suspect alien activity, though no alien life was confirmed.

## 2067 — First Horrox Crew Trains

The first military crew began training for a long-distance human mission to Horrox.

## 2068 — Viajero Launch

The spacecraft **Viajero** launched after approximately one year of intensive training.

The crew was considered expendable and consisted of convicted felons serving life sentences. They were offered a chance at freedom and a new life on Minerva.

The crew used a modified **pHT** to interface with ship systems and, later, the Horrox orbital station.

## 2073-07-06 — Viajero Reaches Minerva

Viajero and its crew docked at the Minerva mining facility.

They found evidence consistent with possible alien activity but did not locate confirmed alien life. The crew restarted the mine, then departed for the Horrox orbital station.

During the journey, Viajero malfunctioned and was drawn toward Horrox by the planet's gravity.

## 2073-07-09 — Contact Lost

Earth lost contact with Viajero and its crew.

## 2076-08-23 — M2 Accelerates

The orbital speed of Horrox's second moon, **M2**, began accelerating.

After several rotations, M2 reached sufficient speed to leave the system on a trajectory described as a slingshot toward Earth.

## 2076-08-31 — Viajero Returns to Contact

Viajero came back online, docked at the Horrox orbital station, and communicated with Earth. The crew warned of M2's movement and its projected course.

> **Archive discrepancy:** The source records Viajero as lost in 2073 and active again in 2076 without explaining the intervening years. No reconciliation is imposed.

## 2078-01-01 — Interception Missiles Launched

Earth launched missiles intended to intercept M2.

## 2081-06-05 — M2 Fragments

As the interception missiles approached, M2 broke into smaller pieces. Most missiles missed their intended targets and the fragments continued toward Earth.

Earth retained nuclear weapons as a backup defense and reconfigured them for area detonations intended to push the fragments off course.

## 2082-03-09 — Nuclear Deflection

The nuclear weapons detonated near the M2 fragments.

Some fragments were diverted toward Mars or Venus, some missed the planets entirely, and others continued toward Earth.

Planetary defenses were insufficient to stop every remaining fragment.

## 2083 — Impact and Technological Collapse

Remaining meteor fragments struck Earth over a period of approximately two months. Some penetrated deep into the crust.

The source attributes a worldwide chain of irregular volcanic eruptions to the impacts. It states that the combined meteor event and global volcanic catastrophe produced an electromagnetic effect powerful enough to destroy electronics and technological systems worldwide.

Human civilization entered a new dark age.

Surviving technology was gradually lost, damaged, or deliberately destroyed by militias and other groups seeking to prevent rivals from holding a technological advantage.

Books and accumulated human knowledge survived in sufficient quantity for technology, electronics, and computers to be rebuilt over time.

This event is indexed separately as **M2 Impact and Technological Collapse** because it is a major continuity anchor for the **Runic Labyrinth** game.

## 2113 — 8086B

The **8086B** computer chip was created and became common in computing devices and handheld computers during the rebuilding period.

## 2138 — Terminal 934 and Gatherers

**Where:** Earth: South Africa

Terminal 934 Model computers were used to deploy **Gatherers**.

Gatherers were small mining drones that searched for, detected, collected, and sold fragments of the meteor impacts to refineries.

Recovered Terminal 934 Model specifications:

- monochrome text-based display
- 32-bit CPU
- 100 MHz clock speed
- 4 GB memory
- handheld form factor for portable drone control

> **Archive discrepancy:** A separate summary in the same source dates merchant use of Terminal 934 to 2038. The century remains unresolved.

## 3201 — Terminal 582 Temporal Deployment Record

The source places a major LightTech temporal deployment in 3201, described elsewhere more generally as the 33rd century.

LightTech placed Terminals inside capsules and buried them in anomalous zones such as the Bermuda Triangle. A popular scientific theory in this source describes such zones as wormholes that defy ordinary definitions of space and time.

Terminal 582 was placed in a classified anomalous area.

**Terminal 582 AI codename:** Prompt

**Recovered prime directive:** Hack and wipe Star Terminal to prevent discovery of planet **M.456**.

> **Archive discrepancy:** Other Final Log material identifies the target planet as **m.471 / Horrox**. M.456 is preserved here because the recovered source uses that designation.

# Major Reconciliation Questions

The following disagreements are intentionally unresolved:

- Was the Star Terminal discovery in 1992, 2016, or formally established only in 2020?
- Why does one 1992 index line name Terminal 582 where another names Star Terminal?
- Is M.456 an obsolete designation, a corrupted copy, or a different world from m.471 / Horrox?
- Did Terminal 934 and the Gatherers operate in 2038, 2138, or both?
- What happened to Viajero between the loss of contact in 2073 and its return in 2076?
- Were the M2 fragments ordinary extraterrestrial material, Chronolith-bearing material, or something else?
- What exact physical mechanism linked the 2083 impacts, volcanic activity, and worldwide technological failure?

The archive will not choose answers merely to make the timeline look clean.
