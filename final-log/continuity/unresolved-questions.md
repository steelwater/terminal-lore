# Unresolved Questions

The Final Log preserves unresolved continuity questions instead of hiding them.

## Current Questions

- What is the exact origin of Chronolith?
- Are anomalous zones natural, artificial, or the result of ancient Chronolith effects?
- What mechanism prevents future recovery of deployed Terminals?
- Did Horrox discovery cause the catastrophe, or only reveal an unavoidable system response?
- What happened to Echo Squad / The Lost Legion?
- When exactly did the Sons of Impact form?
- Are Specters many entities, one entity, projections, witnesses, or something else?
- What is the relationship between the Touched and ordinary witnesses?
- Did Prompt and Vector understand their own historical side effects?

## Runic Labyrinth Questions

- Which parts of the C.A.R.L. Simulator are literal history and which parts are reconstruction or game-like abstraction?
- Was the luminous Orb itself a Terminal using Contextual Phase Adaptation, or a different Chronolith-linked technology?
- What exactly was the glowing Rune or relic recovered beneath St. Roch Cemetery?
- Why were Chronolith shards present beneath St. Roch Cemetery in 1878?
- What is the historical nature of the ghouls and the Demon represented by the simulator?
- How did Carl's story survive after the simulator says stories about the Orb were lost in time?
- Are **One Touched**, **DarkAnal0g**, and **Dark4nal0g** different custodians, different names for the same person, or different layers of the recovered source?
- Is Dark4nal0g one of the Touched?

## Recovered Terminal Timeline Questions

- Was Planet m.471 first discovered by Star Terminal in 1992, 2016, or only formally established with the 2020 public announcement?
- Why does one 1992 source line identify Terminal 582 while another identifies Star Terminal?
- What happened to Viajero between the 2073 loss of contact and its unexplained return in 2076?
- What non-local intelligence, system, or physical process produced the Horrox response associated with Chronolith contamination?
- How did Chronolith excitation translate into M2's orbital acceleration?
- Did the M2 fragments contain Chronolith, Chronolith Resonant material, or unrelated extraterrestrial matter?
- What exact mechanism connected the 2083 impacts, volcanic activity, and worldwide electromagnetic technological failure?
- Did every region experience the post-impact technological collapse equally?
- What became of the early Prompt prototype reportedly tested in the Philippines in 2020?
- What was Terminal 684 / the Mediculo Terminal?
- How do the Patagonia rock art, Mars DNA, non-coding DNA, cockroach, dinosaur, and tongue-covered alien fragments connect to the larger chronology?

## Classification Rule

If a contradiction is useful, preserve it.

If a contradiction blocks story development, mark it for resolution.

> **NULL ARCHIVIST // METHOD**  
> A clean chronology is not evidence of a true chronology. When two surviving sources know different versions of an event, the Final Log records both until the difference itself can be explained.
