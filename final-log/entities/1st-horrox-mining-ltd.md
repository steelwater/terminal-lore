# 1st Horrox Mining Ltd.

**1st Horrox Mining Ltd.** is a **LightTech Corp subsidiary** responsible for LightTech's staffed space-mining activity in the Horrox system.

Its Minerva operation begins on **2075-09-17**, after Viajero restored the older mining infrastructure and before the destabilization of M2.

The company operates a combined mining, refining, grading, storage, maintenance, docking, and export facility. Commercial extraction focuses on Hydronium from the Minerva Cluster, with processed material transported to Mars.

During Hydronium operations, crews gradually discover material later identified as **Chronolith**. Hydronium and Chronolith are distinct materials. The later decision to extract Chronolith becomes part of the causal chain leading toward the Horrox catastrophe.

The precise progression from accidental discovery to deliberate Chronolith mining remains open for future recovered records.
