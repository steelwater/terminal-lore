# 1st Horrox Mining Ltd.

**1st Horrox Mining Ltd.** is a **LightTech Corp subsidiary** responsible for LightTech's staffed space-mining activity in the Horrox system.

Its Minerva operation begins on **2075-09-17**, after Viajero restored the older mining infrastructure and before the destabilization of M2.

The company operates a combined mining, refining, grading, storage, maintenance, docking, and export facility. Commercial extraction focuses on Hydronium from the Minerva Cluster, with processed material transported to Mars.

During Hydronium operations, tiny scanner-invisible fragments of an unknown material are present in some mined asteroids. Repeated excavator faults and interference eventually lead a crew member to suspect that something in particular ore loads is disrupting equipment. Small samples are later isolated and assigned an ordinary mineral/sample number.

Hydronium and this material are distinct. Later surveys find a major concentration of the same numbered mineral on M2. Its identity and properties are still not understood when first classified. Deliberate extraction follows only later.

Later history identifies the material as **Chronolith** and connects its mining to the Horrox catastrophe. The mineral number, discovering crew member, exact M2 deposit, and detailed discovery chronology remain intentionally unresolved.
