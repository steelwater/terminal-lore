# Prompt and Vector

Two known Terminal AI systems have been identified.

## Prompt

Associated with **Terminal 582**.

Primary directive: prevent discovery of Horrox.

LightTech later reclassified Prompt's directive as strategically sound but causally insufficient.

## Vector

Associated with **Terminal 1395**.

Designed to counter Prompt's interventions.

## Classification Note

Both systems interpret directives independently. Conflicts between them may have produced historical side effects not predicted by LightTech.
