# Fran

**Fran** is the later informal name given by the Minerva crew to the mining facility's artificial intelligence.

At the beginning of 1st Horrox Mining Ltd. operations on **2075-09-17**, the system is known simply as **Facility AI / Control**. Fran is therefore not a manufacturer designation, product name, or Launch Day identity.

The nickname develops later through the crew's relationship with the system.

Fran coordinates and reports on facility operations and is notably precise when applying the 1st Horrox Mining Ltd. Operations Handbook. Crew members may paraphrase, misremember, dispute, or joke about procedures; Fran tends to state classifications and requirements technically.

Fran should remain distinct from Terminal-associated AI systems such as Prompt and Vector unless later canon establishes a relationship.
