# Horrox System

Astronomical designation: **m.471**

**Horrox is Planet m.471.**

The planet was discovered by astronomer Horaccio Roxas using the Star Terminal.

## Planet m.471

A rocky exoplanet several light-years from Earth.

Features:

- Mineral-rich crust
- Multiple moons
- No confirmed biological life

Planet m.471 became the primary target for LightTech exploration missions.

## Moon: Minerva

Minerva was selected as the first mining site.

Characteristics:

- Rich in Hydronium Crystallite
- Heavy meteor impact history
- Extensive crater fields

Mining operations on Minerva were fully automated and unmanned.

Ore shipments were transported to Mars for industrial refinement.

## Moon: M2

M2 is the second moon of Planet m.471.

Its orbital velocity later began accelerating in a non-random pattern until it destabilized and left the system.

Later LightTech analysis connected this event to **Chronolith residue propagation beyond Sol**. Residue carried by long-range probes, warp systems, and Minerva mining infrastructure preceded anomalous signal amplification, loss of Minerva communications, M2's orbital acceleration, and broader system destabilization.

LightTech concluded that **the Horrox system responded to Chronolith presence**.

The exact mechanism remains unconfirmed. The leading hypothesis is that a non-local intelligence or system can detect Chronolith excitation and react to it as intrusion, signal, contamination, or an activation request.

Fragments from M2 were later deflected toward Earth, contributing to the global catastrophe.

## Horrox Orbital Station

A large orbital construction platform used to coordinate mining and probe missions.

It served as a relay between Earth, Mars, and the Minerva mining complex.

## The Viajero Incident

The spacecraft Viajero attempted to reach the Horrox orbital station after restoring Minerva operations.

The vessel later malfunctioned and was lost near Planet m.471 shortly before the M2 destabilization event.
